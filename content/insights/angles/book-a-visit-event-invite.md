## How to test it

1. Pick one in-store or online moment you already run, or create a simple one (late opening, demo day, free workshop).
2. Lead with the date and place in the first line of the image or video, and use "Book now" or "Get tickets" as the button.
3. Target people within travelling distance of the venue, and compare cost per booking against your usual cost per store visit or lead.
4. Retarget people who booked with a product ad the week after.
