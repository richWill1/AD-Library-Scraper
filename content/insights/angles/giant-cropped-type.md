## How to test it

1. Take your sale name and set it far bigger than the frame, cropped on at least one edge.
2. Put the dates or the offer small in the centre.
3. Use one brand colour and nothing else.
4. Compare click-through rate against your usual sale banner; follow up with a product ad for people who clicked.
