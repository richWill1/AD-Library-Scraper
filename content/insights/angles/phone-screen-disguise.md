## How to test it

1. Pick the app whose meaning matches your message: Calendar for habits and routines, Notes for lists and offers, toggles for "what is missing", notifications for urgency.
2. Recreate it faithfully at full screen with your product dropped in, and keep any offer inside the app's own text style.
3. Make two versions of the same offer in different apps to see which metaphor works for your audience.
4. Compare thumb-stop rate and click-through against your usual offer graphic.
