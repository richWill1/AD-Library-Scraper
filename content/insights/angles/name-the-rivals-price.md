## How to test it

1. Only compare like for like, with prices you can evidence on the day the ad runs, and keep a dated record (UK comparative advertising rules apply).
2. Show three numbers at most, with yours first and largest.
3. If naming a rival feels too risky, compare against the category instead ("what most Black Friday deals give you").
4. Test against your usual price-led ad and watch cost per purchase, not just clicks.
