## How to test it

1. Pick one hero product with a genuine saving.
2. Add a single sticker in one corner: old price struck through, new price large, and "Save £X" if the saving is big.
3. Test it against the same photo with only a "% off" line.
4. Check that the was-price is a real recent price: UK pricing rules require it.
