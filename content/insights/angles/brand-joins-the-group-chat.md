## How to test it

1. Write a real-sounding thread of three to six messages about the problem your product solves, in the voice your customers use with friends.
2. Bring the product in late, as a photo, a reply or "[Brand] was added to the conversation", and end on a reaction rather than a slogan.
3. Keep the phone furniture (time, names, read receipts) but make sure the brand is obvious within the first second.
4. Test it against your best product image with the same offer and compare thumb-stop rate and cost per click.
