## How to test it

1. Take the offer or promise from your best-selling ad and set it in large type on a single brand colour.
2. Keep it to six words or fewer, and make the logo small.
3. Run it as a 4:5 image and a 9:16 Story against the same offer shown on a product photo.
4. Watch click-through rate and cost per click first; this format often wins attention and loses on conversion, so check both.
