## How to test it

1. Pick a prize that fits your customer, or a free gift they can send to someone else.
2. Show the prize itself, big, with "Win" or "Send" in the first three words.
3. Tie entry to an action you value: a purchase, a sign-up or a store visit (check the rules for prize promotions).
4. Compare cost per entry and the share rate with your usual offer ad.
