## How to test it

1. Ask the local publisher for your area about a branded content package that includes paid Meta promotion from their Page.
2. Use a real local photo with a two-line strap: what to do, and where ("Spend quality time together / this Christmas at Speke Hall").
3. Target a tight radius around the venue or store.
4. Compare cost per visit, booking or store-locator click against the same creative run from your own Page.
