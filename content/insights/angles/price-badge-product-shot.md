## How to test it

1. Choose one hero product with a price people will see as good value.
2. Add a simple badge (price, "Was/Now" or "New") in one corner, the same colour as your sale signage.
3. Test it against the same photo without the badge, with identical copy.
4. Compare cost per purchase; a higher click cost is fine if fewer, better clicks convert.
