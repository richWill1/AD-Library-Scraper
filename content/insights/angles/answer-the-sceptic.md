## How to test it

1. Pull the most common objection from your comments, reviews or customer service inbox, ideally word for word.
2. Pin it on screen in the platform's own comment or question-sticker style for the first two seconds.
3. Have a founder, staff member or customer answer it plainly in under 20 seconds, with one proof point.
4. Run a version per objection and compare hook rate and cost per purchase; the winning objection tells you what to fix on the landing page too.
