## How to test it

1. Write five caption hooks that sound like a person, not a brand: a confession ("I don't know what to buy him"), a POV, or a small story ("Finally convinced Pete to…").
2. Film each on a phone in a real setting, with the product in shot within three seconds.
3. Keep the offer and landing page the same as your current best ad, so the hook is the only thing that changes.
4. Judge on thumb-stop rate (3-second views ÷ impressions) first, then on cost per purchase once each version has spent enough to compare.
