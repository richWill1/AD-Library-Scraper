## How to test it

1. Write the list in real handwriting on real paper and photograph it; scanned fonts look fake.
2. Put two or three everyday items first so your line reads as part of normal life.
3. Make your item the only one that is circled, underlined or ticked.
4. Test against your usual gift or offer ad and compare cost per click and save rate.
