## How to test it

1. Run a lead or sign-up ad one to two weeks before a sale: "Shop before everyone else" plus the date.
2. Keep the creative to one line and one button.
3. Send the early-access email or SMS a few hours before the public sale opens.
4. Judge on cost per sign-up and on revenue per sign-up during the sale.
