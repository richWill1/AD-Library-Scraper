## How to test it

1. Write five captions that sound like a person, not a brand: a POV, a confession or a tiny story.
2. Film each on a phone in a real setting, with the product in shot within three seconds.
3. Keep the offer and landing page the same as your current best ad, so the caption is the only thing that changes.
4. Judge on thumb-stop rate (3-second views ÷ impressions) first, then on cost per purchase.
