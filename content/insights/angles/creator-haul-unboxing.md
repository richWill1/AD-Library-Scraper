## How to test it

1. Brief one or two creators to open a bundle, gift set or "best of" selection on camera in one take.
2. Ask them to say the price or deal out loud and put it on screen.
3. Run the creator's version as a partnership ad alongside a cut of the same footage from your own Page.
4. Compare cost per purchase and average order value, since hauls often sell more than one item.
