## How to test it

1. Write the sale announcement in your brand's most understated voice, one line, no exclamation marks.
2. Put it on a plain background or a simple product close-up, with the discount small or only in the copy.
3. Run it against your standard sale banner with the same offer during the same days.
4. Watch thumb-stop rate and cost per purchase; if it wins on attention but not sales, add the discount to the visual.
