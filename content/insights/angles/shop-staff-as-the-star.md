## How to test it

1. Cast a colleague (or an actor in your real uniform) and film in store on a phone.
2. Give them one simple bit: a quiz, a reaction or a list, with one product per beat.
3. Put the joke or question on screen as a caption for sound-off viewers.
4. Compare thumb-stop rate and cost per purchase against your polished brand video.
