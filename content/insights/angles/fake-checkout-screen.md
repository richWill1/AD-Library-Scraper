## How to test it

1. Rebuild your checkout or basket as a clean graphic: two or three lines, old and new prices, and the total saving.
2. Add one human touch, such as a message notification from a happy customer.
3. Use real prices from your current offer and land people on that exact bundle.
4. Run it against your usual product shot with the same offer and compare cost per purchase and average order value.
