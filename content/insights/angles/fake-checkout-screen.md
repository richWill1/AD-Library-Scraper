## How to test it

1. Rebuild your checkout or basket screen as a clean graphic: two or three products, old and new prices, and the total saving circled.
2. Add one human touch on top, such as a message notification ("Glad we're finally sorting the sound situation").
3. Use real prices from your current offer and keep the landing page on that exact bundle.
4. Run it against your usual product shot with the same offer, and compare cost per purchase and average order value.
