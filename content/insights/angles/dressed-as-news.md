## How to test it

1. Find a story your audience is already talking about this week, or a question they would type into Reddit.
2. Mock up the headline, article or thread cleanly and keep it clearly yours: your brand name or product must be visible.
3. Never fake a real publication's masthead or a real user's post; write your own headline in a generic news style.
4. Compare thumb-stop rate and comments against your usual creative; this format often wins on reach rather than conversion, so judge it on cost per new visitor.
