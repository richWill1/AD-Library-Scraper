Data deletion

How to delete AdSnoop data from your browser, and how to ask us to delete personal data or Meta Platform Data we hold.

Last updated 18 November 2026. The full notice is the privacy policy.

AdSnoop does not give visitors an account. Saved research stays in the browser. Meta Platform Data used by the service is public Ad Library content and public Page metadata, retrieved with the service’s own token. We do not sell Platform Data.

Delete data on your device

  1. In your browser, clear site data for adsnoop.co.uk, or for preview.adsnoop.co.uk if you used the preview. That removes saved ads, notes, pinned competitors, advertiser history, the analytics choice and the waiting-game score.
  2. On adsnoop.co.uk, open Analytics settings in the footer and choose Decline. That stops Google Analytics and deletes cookies named _ga or starting with _ga_ for this site.

Ask AdSnoop to delete data

  1. Email [CONTACT EMAIL] with the subject “Data deletion request”.
  2. Say whether you are a visitor, a Facebook or Instagram Page admin, or contacting us because Meta asked for deletion.
  3. If the request is about a Page or an ad, include the numeric Page id or ad id. Do not send a password or a Meta access token. There is no AdSnoop password.
  4. We will say what personal data we hold about you and delete it. Where the request identifies Platform Data that is personal data about the requester, or Meta instructs us to delete Platform Data, we will delete the matching advertiser-directory entry and stored creative files we can find.
  5. We aim to finish within 30 days and to reply by email. We will tell you if we need more detail, or if the law requires us to keep something.

Public ads can remain in Meta’s Ad Library after we delete our copy. To remove an ad or a Page from Facebook or Instagram, use Meta’s own tools. Deleting our copy does not delete Meta’s.

The operator’s encrypted Meta token is not visitor data. Visitors cannot delete it from this page. The operator removes it from the private hosting configuration.
